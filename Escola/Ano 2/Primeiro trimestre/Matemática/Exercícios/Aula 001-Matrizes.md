# Exercício 3
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
0&2\\
-1&3
\end{pmatrix}~e~ B=\begin{pmatrix}
2&4\\
2&5
\end{pmatrix}
$$
Resolva:
a)
$$
\begin{cases}
X+Y=2A\\
X-Y=A-B
\end{cases}
$$
b)
$$

3X-Y=A+3B\\
